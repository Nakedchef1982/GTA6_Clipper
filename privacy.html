Privacy Policy - GTA6 Clipper

Last updated: 2026-11-18

GTA6 Clipper is a single-user desktop application operated by its owner on their own computer.

1. Data we access

When the owner connects their TikTok account, the app receives an access token and a refresh token and the basic profile information permitted by the scope user.info.basic. It uses the video.publish scope solely to upload videos to the owner's own account.

2. How data is used

Tokens are used only to upload videos created by the app to the owner's account. No data is used for advertising, analytics, profiling or sale.

3. Storage

Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer in a private folder. They are not uploaded to any server operated by us and are not shared with third parties.

4. Sharing

We do not share, sell or transfer TikTok user data to anyone.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens remain on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them. The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy > Security & permissions > Manage app permissions, and can delete the local token file.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children and is not offered to the public.
